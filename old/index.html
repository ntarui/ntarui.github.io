About

My research and teaching sit at the intersection of environmental, energy, and international economics — recently, sea-level-rise risk and coastal property values, beach and recreation valuation using mobile-phone mobility data, tariffs and trade, solar and electricity markets, and emissions and productivity in manufacturing microdata.
